Het Steen
2016

The renovation of "Het Steen" visualized for the city of Antwerp. The project includes an AR(augmented reality) experience wich explores the different stages of the renovation process as a series of animations. Visiting hours are from 10h to 17h at "Steenplein 1 2000 Antwerp"
